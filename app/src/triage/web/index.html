      Policy

      Your rules for what should happen with your email. The policy stays on this computer.

      When this is on, the model on this computer reads each new message that arrives in your inbox and carries out the rule that applies, if any. What it did appears in the conversation “Automatic actions” and under Activity, where it can be undone.
